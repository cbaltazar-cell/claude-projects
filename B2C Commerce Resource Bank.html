Catalog & Products Organize what you sell — catalogs, categories, products, price books & inventory

The merchandising foundation of a B2C storefront: how product data is structured, priced, and stocked. Grouped here into Catalog Structure (catalogs, categories, products, variations & options), Pricing (price books plus future-price preview), Data Operations (catalog import/export & feeds), and Inventory. Merchandisers and Admins own most of this in Business Manager; developers surface it through the storefront and APIs. Deeper self-paced learning lives in the Set Up Merchandising for Your Storefront trail.

Search & Findability Help shoppers find products — index, refinements, sorting, dictionaries & SEO

Once products are catalogued, shoppers have to find them. This is native B2C Commerce search & findability, grouped into Indexing & Facets (the search index and faceted refinements), Relevance Tuning (rule-based sorting and native linguistic dictionaries), and External Findability (SEO) — owned mostly by merchandisers and admins in Business Manager. Two AI-driven search layers sit above it and come in the Einstein & AI / Agentforce wave: the classic Einstein features (Predictive Sort, Einstein Search Dictionaries, Search Recommendations) that augment native search, and Agentic Commerce Search (ACS) — an add-on that replaces native keyword search with a trained AI model (native category navigation, content search, and refinements still apply). Everything below is the native foundation ACS and Einstein build on.
